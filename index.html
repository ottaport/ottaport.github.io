About Us

Ottaport is an independent creative studio for films, animation, stories and visual work founded by Milan Naik.

A studio built around stories

Ottaport brings original ideas, characters and visual worlds together under one home. The work can live across film, animation and other screen-based formats without losing a clear studio identity.
